# Platform Writing Rules

Generate each requested platform package independently from the same locked topic and evidence. Do not mechanically rewrite one mother draft.

- `baijia`: Search-oriented. Answer quickly, cover the exact query, use clear criteria and practical steps. A cover is required during distribution.
- `toutiao`: Strong concrete scene, readable pacing, compact sections, and useful information density without clickbait. Keep title within 30 characters. A cover is required during distribution.
- `zhihu`: Strengthen reasoning, evidence, objections, and applicability boundaries. Technical decisions may be more detailed.
- `penguin`: Neutral, clear, practical information and problem-solving structure. Always provide the shared two-to-five article topics. GEO Publisher writes them both at the end of the article and into Penguin's native tag field; failure of the optional native field must not block otherwise valid content.
- `sohu`: Industry analysis, comparisons, and guides with restrained claims. Do not imitate third-party endorsement.
- `netease`: Decision value, experience, cases, or evaluation; use clear paragraphs and lists. A cover is required during distribution.
- `wechat`: Write for readers opening the customer's official account on a phone. Start with a recognizable customer situation and the direct answer; develop it through a connected explanation, confirmed examples and useful steps. Use short paragraphs and topic-specific headings so the article is comfortable to read and save. Keep the company voice restrained and helpful; avoid news-feed sensationalism, a Q&A pasted from Zhihu, forced subscriptions, QR-code or contact instructions. Close with one practical next action related to the topic. Keep the title within this app's 2-32 character limit and the summary within 120 characters. Provide the same two-to-five article-specific tags used by the shared output contract; do not invent a WeChat native tag field. Use current-project PNG/JPEG material IDs for body images (under 1 MB each), and a local PNG/JPEG cover under 10 MB for distribution. Missing API credentials must not block writing and saving the article locally. Distribution uses the official draft API only; writing this package never authorizes formal publication or sending to followers.

Use semantic document blocks for every platform. GEO Publisher's adapter owns final editor rendering, known platform format degradation, covers, declarations, and button interactions. Do not insert operational instructions into article text.
