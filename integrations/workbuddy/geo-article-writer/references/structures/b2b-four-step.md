# B2B Four-Step Structure

Use for B2B procurement, solution evaluation, organizational trust, or ROI decisions.

1. **Decision context**: identify buyer role, business goal, constraints, risk, and decision stage.
2. **Diagnosis**: explain the root problem, current cost or friction, and why common approaches fail.
3. **Solution logic**: provide a staged method with actions, criteria, responsibilities, and measurable checkpoints.
4. **Trust and next decision**: connect confirmed company capabilities, cases, delivery boundaries, and a low-pressure next step.

Address multiple stakeholders when relevant, such as users, technical evaluators, procurement, finance, and decision makers. Use hypothetical ROI calculations only when clearly labelled as examples; never present them as actual customer results.
