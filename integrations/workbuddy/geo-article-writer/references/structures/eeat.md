# E-E-A-T Structure

Use for technical judgment, standards, principles, parameters, or evidence-heavy decisions.

1. Answer the locked question directly and state applicable conditions.
2. Explain the governing principles, standards, parameters, or authoritative basis.
3. Provide an executable evaluation method and verifiable criteria.
4. Use a table only when reliable comparable data exists.
5. Add mechanisms, scenarios, examples, or a checklist when external data is unavailable; never disguise examples as official statistics.
6. Connect confirmed company practice and facts naturally.
7. Explain genuine limits and a practical next step.

Prioritize verifiability and citation value. Do not turn the title into an abstract trend article.
