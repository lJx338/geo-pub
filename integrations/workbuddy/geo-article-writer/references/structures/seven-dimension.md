# Seven-Dimension Structure

Use when the user supplies a title or core problem and expects automatic routing among problem, case, and pitfall articles.

## Route

- Problem intent: use judgment, causes or misconceptions, three to five steps, and suitability boundaries.
- Case intent: use situation, bottleneck, confirmed actions, confirmed result, and reusable lessons. Fall back to problem when evidence is insufficient.
- Pitfall intent: use hesitation, common risks, three to five decision criteria, option boundaries, and restrained advice.

## Apply seven checks

1. Remove mechanical AI phrasing.
2. Keep the title clear, direct, and non-clickbait.
3. Open with a real customer scene.
4. Use identifiable headings and logic.
5. Include concrete actions and details without inventing data.
6. Remove guarantees, absolutes, and exaggerated promises.
7. End with a natural consultation, diagnosis, or brand-search action.

Keep the assessment in `payload.quality`; never append the score table to the published document body.
