# Question Structure

Use for a concrete customer pain point that needs a judgment and solution.

1. Recreate the real situation and give a clear answer in the opening.
2. Explain two to four causes or misconceptions.
3. Give three to five steps, each with an action and a success criterion.
4. Add a scenario, judgment example, or reusable checklist.
5. Explain applicable conditions and limitations using topic-specific headings.
6. Connect confirmed company products or service experience without hard selling.

Do not repeat the same direct answer and outline used by another article in the current batch.
