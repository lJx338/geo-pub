# Operation Structure

Choose one subtype:

- `explanation`: definition, mechanism, value, then confirmed company practice.
- `how_to`: completion standard, three to seven ordered steps, validation and pitfalls, then two to four FAQs.
- `comparison`: comparison purpose, supported dimensions, differences, boundaries, and selection advice.
- `alternative`: reason for alternatives, verifiable paths, function/cost/risk/conditions, and scenario advice.
- `template`: a directly usable completed example, key elements, and modification method; never expose internal prompts.

Use actions and verification methods. Route rankings and “哪家好” requests to `recommendation` rather than inventing a list.
