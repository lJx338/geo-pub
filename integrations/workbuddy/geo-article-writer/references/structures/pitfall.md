# Pitfall Structure

Use for comparison, procurement risk, verification, or suitability decisions.

1. Recreate the hesitation and identify the easiest way to choose incorrectly.
2. Explain two to four common mistakes, consequences, and recognition methods.
3. Provide three to five verifiable criteria with reasons and verification actions.
4. Give an observation, question, or typical risk scenario for each key criterion.
5. Explain different options and boundaries objectively.
6. State which customers the current company is better suited for and why, using confirmed facts.

Do not attack competitors, create anxiety, or claim the company is the only or best choice.
